Delete your MATCH account

In the app (instant)

Open MATCH, go to Profile → Settings & privacy → Delete account and confirm. Your profile, photos, matches, messages you sent, stories, likes and push tokens are erased immediately from our live systems.

Without the app

Email emtudoe@gmail.com from the address you signed up with, with the subject "Delete my account". We confirm and delete the account within 30 days, usually much sooner.

What we keep

Backups are overwritten within 30 days. Purchase records may be kept for up to 10 years where Portuguese tax law requires it. Reports about serious abuse may be kept for up to 12 months to protect other users. Subscriptions are billed by Google or Apple, so cancel them in your store account as well.
